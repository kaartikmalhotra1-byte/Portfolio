About me

I'm working as Lead Product Designer at Monotype. I lead design for how our fonts reach designers, across a desktop app, an Adobe plugin, a Chrome extension and an agentic taskbar, and the licensing systems behind them.

I studied computer science, then spent my early years designing tools that came down to one problem: making processes people can't see trustworthy.

I work on the same problem now, with a model in the loop. I care about where a model should act, where it should stay out, and how it tells you when it's unsure.
