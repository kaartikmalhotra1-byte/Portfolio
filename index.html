HAI

Catching brand breaches on the canvas, not on the printed proof.

A global publisher found the wrong typeface only on a printed draft: 90+ hours of rework and a reprint. I designed a check that runs as designers work in Adobe. Rules and a classifier handle the clear cases, and a language model handles only the ambiguous ones, saying so when it’s unsure. In development

₹70 lakh a month was disappearing. Nobody would say how.
